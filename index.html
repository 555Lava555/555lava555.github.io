Paper Lab Email Alerts is the email-notification component of a privately operated personal paper-research application. It is not a public trading platform or an investment advisory service.

What the application does

Paper Lab studies software for monitoring, accounting and recovery in a personal research environment. Its email component uses a deliberately connected Google account to send fixed operational test or failure notices to that same account, only within the owner's approved workflow.

The qualified flow uses Google sign-in to confirm the selected account and requests permission to send mail. It does not request Gmail inbox-reading, message-deletion or mailbox-management permissions.

What this website does not do

This is an information site. It has no sign-in, credential-entry form, brokerage connection, order control, payment flow or account-registration service. Never submit passwords, API keys or trading/account information here.

Ongoing alerts and unattended operation are not enabled by publishing this site. Configuration binding and test delivery are not proof of current authorization, recovery readiness or safe trading.

Understand the data connection

The privacy notice describes account verification, send-only permission, local protected storage and GitHub Pages hosting. The terms of use describe the private research scope and its limitations.

For questions, use the owner/support email shown on the application's Google consent screen. Do not send credentials or confidential records.

This public information site does not establish domain verification, Google app verification, current authorization or trading readiness. It does not activate alerts or grant account access.
