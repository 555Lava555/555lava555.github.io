Privacy notice

This notice describes the current personal Windows email qualification workflow and static information site. It does not describe an activated server or public multi-user service.

Data accessed and why

The application requests Google OpenID account identification, the account email address and Gmail send-only permission. It verifies that the authorized account matches the owner's selected account. Access and refresh tokens allow approved requests without collecting the account password.

Approved email notices use a fixed test/operational template and opaque fictional-event reference, sent from and to the same selected account. The qualified email branch does not read inbox messages, attachments or contacts, delete messages or manage the mailbox. This website has no Google authorization flow and never asks for tokens or keys.

Storage and retention

The local Windows qualification workflow protects configuration and saved authorization with current-user Windows encryption and restrictive filesystem permissions. It retains consumed attempt records and encrypted evidence to prevent silent replay. Encryption is not a guarantee against a compromised device or authorized account.

No automatic deletion schedule is claimed. Authorization and historical records remain until the owner follows a separately reviewed removal procedure; access may expire or be revoked earlier. Local records are not published with this website. A future server migration or different retention policy requires revised disclosure and review.

Use and sharing

Google processes account-verification and authorized sending requests and stores mail according to the account's Gmail service. The local email connector does not sell Google user data, use it for advertising or send Google identity, authorization tokens or Gmail data to AI models for training or analysis.

The separate phone-alert channel uses its own provider/configuration; Google credentials and identity evidence are not passed to that phone provider. This notice does not authorize any new transfer or provider activity.

Paper Lab Email Alerts' use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements. This is not a claim of Google verification.

Website visitors and hosting

These pages use no scripts, analytics, advertising pixels, forms or application-set cookies. This information site is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub logs visitors' IP addresses for security purposes under its own privacy statement. Following an external link is governed by that destination's practices.

Your control and questions

The owner can revoke the app's Google access through Google Account connection settings. Revocation stops future use of that grant but does not itself remove historical local attempt records or already-sent mail. Contact the owner via the support email shown on the Google consent screen for a reviewed local removal or correction request; never share credentials.

Any material change in permissions, recipients, hosting or data use must be disclosed before the new use is enabled.

Effective 12 October 2026. No current credentials, provider availability, Google verification or unattended operation are certified here.
